## The problem

Large organisations keep most of what they know in unstructured form: documents, records and knowledge spread across teams and systems. People spend a lot of time finding and reconnecting it, and new tools have nothing reliable to stand on.

## My role

I am an AI engineer in a large programme team at Deloitte Singapore. I help build the solution, with a focus on the front end: the screens people work in, shaped by what the client needs from them. The ontology, the graph and the wider programme are the work of the whole team.

## Approach

- **Understand the information first.** How the organisation's knowledge is actually produced, used and connected.
- **Model it.** An ontology that describes the organisation's concepts, and a knowledge graph that holds the information in that shape.
- **Make it usable.** A knowledge base enriched by the graph, with screens designed around the client's requirements. This is where most of my own work is.
- **Build on the foundation.** The graph is meant to support downstream tools, such as conversational applications and bespoke business interfaces that speed up specific processes.

## Status

Ongoing. The foundation is being built now, and the downstream applications are still to come. This page will say more when there is more that can be said.
